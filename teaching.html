Teaching Philosophy

I believe marketing education should empower students to become ethical, socially responsible leaders who understand the profound impact of consumption on people and planet. My approach integrates cutting-edge research with real-world applications, creating learning experiences that are both intellectually rigorous and personally transformative.

I design courses that bridge marketing theory with sustainability practice, encouraging students to question assumptions, engage with complex problems, and develop solutions that create value for business and society.
